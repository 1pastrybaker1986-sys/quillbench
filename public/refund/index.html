Refund policy

Full refund before work starts

Full refund before work starts. After that, refunds are case by case.

Work starts on your clock-start date. Your clock starts on the day Quillbench emails to confirm your brief is complete (all 5 fields filled in: email, package, book title, brief, and files link, and your files link opens). Quillbench sends that email within 1 business day of getting a complete brief. For a Cover Check, your clock starts on the day Quillbench emails to confirm it has your cover, genre and blurb. Cover Check has two promises that are stated exceptions to "case by case": a full refund if you get fewer than 3 usable fixes, and a refund if it's late (see Delivery times).

Cover Design first-look promise

Cover Design first-look promise, a stated exception to "case by case": if you don't like the first version (ebook front cover, audiobook cover and 3D mockup) that Quillbench delivers, email Quillbench (address on our Refunds page) within 7 days of the day Quillbench emails you the first version, before you ask for any changes, and you get a full refund of your $99. Quillbench refunds the full $99 to your original payment method within 5 business days of your email; your bank may take a few more days to show it. You don't need to give a reason. Asking for a change ends the first-look promise. If you take the refund, you don't get the right to use that cover or its files. Quillbench will never show or sell that cover. We may use the same general style for other books, but never your title, your brief, or that cover's artwork. This applies to the $99 Cover Design only, not the Studio Bundle or Print Wrap.

How to ask for a refund

Email sarah@brundigebusiness.com. Include the email address you used at checkout and what you bought (Cover Design, Print Wrap, Studio Bundle or Cover Check).

The refund goes back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What it covers

This applies to Cover Design ($99), the Print Wrap add-on ($59), the Studio Bundle ($499) and the Cover Check ($29).

Upgrading from Cover Design to the Studio Bundle means paying the difference between what you paid and the current Studio Bundle price.

Delivery times

Cover Design is delivered within 5 business days of your clock-start date. The Studio Bundle is delivered within 14 business days of your clock-start date. Print Wrap is delivered within 3 business days; its clock starts once we have your final front cover and final page count. If your Cover Design, Print Wrap or Studio Bundle is late, you get an apology and one extra round of changes, free. If your brief is missing, Quillbench follows up on day 2 and day 5, then the delivery clock pauses until it's complete. Details are on the support page.

A Cover Check is delivered within 5 business days of its clock-start date. If your Cover Check is late, your $29 is refunded and you still get your Check. Cover Check promise: at least 3 fixes you can use, or a full refund. A usable fix is a specific, actionable change to the title, type, image, layout or color. If you get fewer than 3, email sarah@brundigebusiness.com within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
