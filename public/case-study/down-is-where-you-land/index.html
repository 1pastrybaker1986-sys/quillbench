One book, every step: how Down Is Where You Land went from file to Amazon

A near-future Mars survival novel by Arteleus Solarian. Down Is Where You Land is Quillbench's own launch, published under a pen name. Quillbench worked on the cover brief, the cover, the blurb and the KDP setup. Here's each step, the real drafts, and what each step costs if you want it for your book.

Step 1. The cover brief: decide what the cover has to say before anyone designs it

The brief answered one question first: what must a reader know in one second on a crowded shelf? For this book: realistic Mars survival, near future. Everything else followed from that, including a title big enough to read at thumbnail size.

Get this for your book: free Cover Brief tool

Step 2. Three concepts, one winner, four versions

We made three directions, picked one, then took it through four versions. Each version was checked at 150 pixels wide, and the later versions were placed next to current bestsellers.

How long it took: Our founder made this cover around her full-time job, with 3 rounds of changes on our own book. A paid Cover Design still delivers your first version within 5 business days.

Some covers are made with AI tools and some by hand. Either way, Quillbench reviews and edits every cover before it's delivered.

Step 3. The blurb: every claim checked against the story

Two lengths, 151 and 98 words. Every plot point was checked against the outline, so the blurb never promises something the book doesn't deliver.

Get this for your book: blurb polish, included in the Studio Bundle

Step 4. Keywords and pricing: sourced, not guessed

Seven keyword boxes, each with a reason and a source. Title words were kept out of the boxes. Price and categories were checked against current comps.

Step 5. Live

Paperback and Kindle, October 20. Paperback, 350 pages, Book One of a six-book series.

FAQ

What's the difference between a Cover Check, a Cover Design and the Studio Bundle?

A Cover Check is $29: a written critique of the cover you already have, so you know what's working, what's holding it back, and what to fix first. A Cover Design is $99: one new cover concept for your book, with an ebook front cover, a 2400x2400 audiobook cover and a 3D book mockup. The Studio Bundle is $499: the cover, a print wrap (spine and back), a proofread up to 40,000 words, blurb polish and 6 promo graphics sized from your approved cover.

How fast will I get it?

Cover Design is delivered within 5 business days of your clock-start date. The Studio Bundle is delivered within 14 business days of your clock-start date. Your clock starts the day Quillbench emails to confirm your brief is complete (all 5 fields filled in and your files link opens), within 1 business day. A Cover Check is delivered within 5 business days of its clock-start date, which is the day Quillbench emails to confirm it has your cover, genre and blurb. Print Wrap is delivered within 3 business days. Its clock starts once we have your final front cover and your final page count.

What if I don't like it?

Cover Design includes 2 rounds of changes. The Studio Bundle includes 5. Misses from the brief are fixed free and don't count as a round. Print Wrap includes 1 revision and a free spine redo.

Some covers are made with AI tools and some by hand. Either way, Quillbench reviews and edits every cover before it's delivered. Cover Check is drafted with AI tools, then edited and signed off by Quillbench.

Do I need to disclose AI to KDP?

KDP asks about AI-generated text, images and translations when you publish. Quillbench never uses AI to write your text. Cover images may use AI tools minimally, and Quillbench reviews and edits them by hand. If any part of your cover uses AI-generated imagery, select that option on KDP's Content page.
