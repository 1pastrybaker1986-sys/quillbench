Some covers are made with AI tools and some by hand. Either way, Quillbench reviews and edits every cover before it's delivered.

Paid unlocks use Stripe Checkout. After a successful payment, the unlock is saved on this device only. It doesn't carry over to other phones or computers automatically, even if you're signed in.

Delivery

Cover Design is delivered within 5 business days of your clock-start date. The Studio Bundle is delivered within 14 business days of your clock-start date. Your clock starts the day Quillbench emails to confirm your brief is complete (all 5 fields filled in and your files link opens), within 1 business day. Work starts on your clock-start date.

If your brief is missing or incomplete, Quillbench follows up by email on day 2 and day 5. After that, the delivery clock is paused until your brief is complete.

If Quillbench delivers your Cover Design, Print Wrap or Studio Bundle late, you get an apology and one extra round of changes, free.

Print Wrap is delivered within 3 business days. Its clock starts once we have your final front cover and your final page count.

Cover Design includes 2 rounds of changes. The Studio Bundle includes 5. Misses from the brief are fixed free and don't count as a round. Print Wrap includes 1 revision and a free spine redo.

You can upgrade from Cover Design ($99) to the Studio Bundle by paying the difference between what you paid and the current Studio Bundle price.
